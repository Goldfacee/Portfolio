Research

Research & Development

My research work focuses on autonomous flight, control systems, robotics, and simulation-based testing. I’m currently involved in two NJIT labs, where I’m learning how engineering ideas move from papers and simulations into real-world systems.

NJIT Networking Research Lab

Networking Research Lab

Autonomous Flight Path Planning Energy Efficiency

I’m an Undergraduate Research Lead at NJIT’s Networking Research Lab, where our work goes beyond traditional networking and into areas like energy-efficient autonomous flight. Our team published “EcoFlight: Low-Energy Path Finding Through Obstacles for Autonomous Sensing Drones,” where we developed an algorithm that helps drones choose obstacle-free paths while using less energy. After validating the idea in simulation, we’re now working on bringing it into real flight by building a custom drone platform and testing the hardware step by step. It has been a challenging but exciting process, and each test flight gets us closer to a drone that can better understand and navigate the world around it.

NJIT Controls, Automation, and Robotics Lab

Controls, Automation, and Robotics Lab

Robotics Robotic Hands Control Systems

My interest in control systems led me to NJIT’s Controls, Automation, and Robotics Lab, where I work as an undergraduate research assistant under a PhD candidate. Our research focuses on dexterous in-hand object manipulation for humanoid robotic hands — one of the big challenges standing between today’s robots and robots that can casually make you an omelet or fold your laundry. My work includes literature review, simulation-based testing with robotic hands, and method verification as we continue developing the project toward a future paper.
